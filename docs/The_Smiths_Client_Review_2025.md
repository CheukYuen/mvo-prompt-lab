## 6. Key Talking Points for Meeting

### A. Portfolio Performance

- Review full-year 2025 returns vs. the blended benchmark

- Discuss any laggards and whether repositioning is warranted

### B. Allocation & Risk

- **Flag the moderate risk tolerance vs. 70% equity:** With moderate risk appetite and 10 years to retirement, discuss whether current equity overweight feels comfortable during market downturns
- Introduce the concept of a glide path toward retirement
- Review the cash position — 10% may be too high if not serving a specific purpose; could be redeployed into short-duration bonds for better yield

### C. Retirement Planning

- Confirm annual savings/contribution rate — this is the #1 lever
- Set a retirement income target and work backwards to required portfolio size
- Discuss Social Security timing strategy

### D. Planning & Life Events

- Any major expenses anticipated in the next 1–3 years?
- Estate planning / beneficiary designations up to date?
- Tax efficiency: Are tax-advantaged accounts (401k, IRA, Roth) being maximized?
